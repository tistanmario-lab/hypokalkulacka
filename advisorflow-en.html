Advisor Flow – Less administration.
More room for business.

An intelligent sales assistant for financial intermediaries and sales teams.

Advisor Flow is an upcoming iOS app that helps simplify sales processes, organise meetings, manage tasks and track sales performance.

I'm interested in testing See the features

The app is under development and is not yet publicly available.

Problem and solution

All important sales activities in one place.

A salesperson loses time not while selling, but around it. These are situations we know from everyday work in financial intermediation.

Scattered information

Contacts, notes, calls and appointments live in different apps and notebooks.

Manual call logging

After every call you have to find the contact and write down by hand how it went.

Forgotten follow-ups

A promised call-back or next step easily gets lost in the rush of the day.

Inefficient calendar work

Booking, moving or cancelling an appointment means jumping between several places.

Little insight into performance

Without numbers it is hard to know how many calls lead to a meeting and how many meetings lead to a deal.

Advisor Flow aims to bring these things together into one smooth working day.

The goal is for one step to follow naturally from the next, so the advisor does not have to retype anything or keep it all in their head.

Call management

Log a call result in a few taps, a “Who to call today” list and tracking of call-backs.

In development

Contact management

Import of contacts from the iPhone, statuses from lead to client, and a history of calls and meetings for every contact.

In development

Planning sales meetings

Meetings are written to Apple Calendar. Moving or cancelling an appointment is reflected in the calendar event too.

In development

Reminders and work tasks

Follow-ups with notifications, and tasks that can be written to Apple Reminders, including for a colleague.

In development

Follow-up communication automation

The app suggests the next step and prepares the message text. The user always confirms sending. Broader automation is planned.

Statistics and performance review

Apple ecosystem integration

Calendar, Reminders, Contacts and the system Messages and Mail apps. We are also preparing an Apple Watch companion and a Mac app.

About the startup

About Advisor Flow

Building the future of sales productivity.

Advisor Flow is a new, self-funded B2B SaaS initiative started in Slovakia in 2026.

Built by a financial services professional, for professionals who understand that every minute spent on administration is a minute away from clients. It grows out of real, everyday sales practice rather than a theoretical concept.

We're building a native iOS application to streamline calls, appointments, follow-ups, tasks and performance analytics for financial advisors and sales teams.

The product is currently under active development.

We plan to validate it through internal testing with an established network of financial advisors before launching a commercial subscription model.

We use Claude Code to support product development and plan to integrate Claude API to build secure, responsible AI-powered productivity features, such as intelligent summaries, meeting preparation and workflow automation.

Our long-term vision is to provide an intelligent, scalable productivity platform for independent professionals and businesses.

Founder background

The founder has worked in financial services for approximately ten years and has operated as a sole proprietor since December 2021.

Advisor Flow is a new software product initiative, not a separately incorporated company.
